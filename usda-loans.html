Loan program overview

USDA Loans

USDA loans support eligible primary-residence financing in qualifying areas and are subject to household-income limits, property eligibility, occupancy rules, and lender requirements.

Discuss This ProgramCompare All Programs
LocationEligible areas only
IncomeHousehold limits apply
OccupancyPrimary residence

Who this program may fit

  • Eligible households buying in qualifying areas
  • Primary-residence buyers who meet income and program requirements
  • Borrowers comparing zero-down possibilities

Potential benefits

  • May offer zero-down financing for eligible borrowers and properties
  • Fixed-rate financing may be available
  • Can support homeownership in qualifying rural and suburban areas

Important considerations

  • Household income limits generally apply
  • Property location must be eligible
  • Guarantee fees may apply
  • Program funding, availability, and lender requirements can change

What a review may include

A mortgage review commonly considers income, employment, assets, credit history, monthly obligations, occupancy, property type, purchase price or value, available funds, and applicable program requirements.
