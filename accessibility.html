Our accessibility goal

Right Away Lending aims to provide a website that is usable by people with a wide range of abilities and assistive technologies.

Measures included

  • Keyboard-accessible controls
  • Visible focus indicators
  • Semantic headings and landmarks
  • Reduced-motion support
  • Responsive layouts and readable contrast
  • Alternative text for meaningful images

Feedback

Accessibility feedback may be submitted by phone at (602) 465-2800. You can also email djaramillo@rightawaylending.com. Please describe the page and the assistance you need without including sensitive financial information.
