Website Privacy Notice

How this website handles browsing information, planning-tool inputs, and requests to contact Right Away Lending Corp

Scope of this notice

This notice describes this public website and its local planning tools. Right Away Lending Corp is identified by company NMLS #2412327. This notice does not replace any financial privacy notice, disclosures, or privacy rights that apply when you request or obtain a financial product or service. Ask the team for the applicable financial privacy notice before sharing financial documents.

Planning tools and your entries

The inquiry worksheet, program explorer, calculators, glossary search, and document checklist run in your browser. The website does not transmit their entries to Right Away Lending, a lender, a credit bureau, or an analytics service. They do not submit an application, request credit, book an appointment, or provide consent to marketing.

The website does not save worksheet answers or calculator inputs in local storage, cookies, or an account. Your browser may retain page history or restore form values. Use the worksheet’s Clear Worksheet button and close the page when you finish, especially on a shared device. Printed or saved reports remain wherever you choose to save them.

When you contact the team

Calling or emailing opens your phone or email application. You choose whether to place the call or send a message. Information you provide through those channels is received by the company and the communication providers involved, rather than by the local worksheet. Use these channels to ask a general question or arrange a conversation. Contacting us does not book an appointment until the team confirms it.

Do not send Social Security numbers, account numbers, passwords, tax returns, credit reports, or financial documents through ordinary email or this website. Ask the team for an approved secure channel and the applicable financial privacy notice. The website does not include document upload or an online loan application.

Browser storage, hosting, and diagnostics

The website may save your light or dark appearance preference in your browser under ral-theme. Public website files may also be cached for offline access; worksheet answers are not included in that cache. You can remove these files through your browser’s site-data controls. Blocking storage does not prevent access to the guides or contact links.

When you request a page, hosting, delivery, and security services receive technical information such as your IP address, requested URL, browser information, and request time. Logs and network-error reports may be processed according to the provider’s configuration and policies to deliver and protect the site. The site code includes no advertising pixels, behavioral analytics, or session recording.

External websites and your choices

Links to NMLS Consumer Access, regulators, educational resources, or other websites take you to independent services with their own privacy practices. A link does not transfer worksheet answers. You can use the public guides without entering personal information, leave optional worksheet fields blank, clear local site data, or contact us directly.

For questions about information shared with the company, retention, disclosures to other parties, or applicable privacy rights, contact the team and request its financial privacy notice. This website notice does not limit rights available under applicable law.

Contact and updates

Privacy questions: (602) 465-2800 or djaramillo@rightawaylending.com. Mailing address: Right Away Lending Corp, 4001 N 3rd St, Suite 460, Phoenix, AZ 85012. Do not include sensitive financial details in an initial message.

Last updated September 18, 2026. Changes to contact, analytics, application, or hosting services require a corresponding update to this notice before those services are enabled.
