# Right Away Lending Design System — Version 17

## Purpose

This guide documents the reusable visual and interaction system used across the Right Away Lending website.

## Brand colors

- Navy: `#002244`
- Support Blue: `#245998`
- Action Green: `#69BE28`
- Dark Green: `#51A616`
- Wolf Gray: `#A5ACAF`
- Ice White: `#F8FAFC`
- Charcoal: `#1E293B`

## Typography hierarchy

- Eyebrow: uppercase, compact, green accent
- H1: hero and page title
- H2: major section title
- H3: card and component title
- Lead: introductory paragraph
- Body: educational and informational copy
- Disclaimer: small, muted compliance copy

## Buttons

- Primary: green background, white text
- Secondary: white or surface background with navy border
- Light: transparent with white border on dark backgrounds
- Text links: blue or green with arrow treatment

## Cards

- Standard radius: 20px
- Premium panel radius: 28px
- Border: 1px solid system line color
- Hover: subtle lift and medium shadow
- Icons: flat, single-color, section-appropriate

## Spacing

The spacing scale is based on 4px increments. Most layouts use:

- 8px
- 12px
- 16px
- 24px
- 32px
- 48px
- 64px

## Accessibility

- Minimum touch target: 44px
- Visible keyboard focus
- Reduced-motion support
- High-contrast text on dark surfaces
- Semantic headings
- Accessible labels on interactive controls

## Responsive behavior

- Mobile: up to 700px
- Tablet: 701px–1100px
- Desktop: above 1100px
