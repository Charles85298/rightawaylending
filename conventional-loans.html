Loan program overview

Conventional Loans

Conventional financing is not insured by FHA, guaranteed by VA, or guaranteed by USDA. It can support a wide range of purchase and refinance goals, subject to lender and investor requirements.

Discuss This ProgramCompare All Programs
OccupancyPrimary, second home, some investment
InsuranceMay apply based on structure
LimitsConforming limits may apply

Who this program may fit

  • Primary residence purchases
  • Second-home financing
  • Certain investment-property transactions
  • Borrowers comparing mortgage-insurance structures

Potential benefits

  • Broad product availability
  • Potential options for multiple occupancy types
  • Mortgage insurance may be removable when applicable requirements are met
  • Fixed-rate and adjustable-rate structures may be available

Important considerations

  • Qualification, pricing, and mortgage insurance vary by credit, down payment, property, and other factors
  • Low-down-payment programs may include income or first-time-buyer rules
  • Conforming loan limits and property eligibility apply

What a review may include

A mortgage review commonly considers income, employment, assets, credit history, monthly obligations, occupancy, property type, purchase price or value, available funds, and applicable program requirements.
